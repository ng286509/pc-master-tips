Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This website is designed to provide educational content. It does not currently provide a user account, registration form, or on-site contact form. Do not send private information through public comments or messages.

The website owner has not declared any analytics or advertising service for this site in this policy. GitHub Pages and the visitor's browser may process technical information as part of delivering and viewing the website. Check the relevant provider policies for details.

Pages may link to external websites and services. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. PC Master Tips does not control third-party websites.

The educational content is intended for general learners. Do not submit personal details, passwords, OTPs or other sensitive information to the website or to unknown third parties.

5. Security and Responsible Use

Keep your devices and accounts secure. Tutorials are for education and authorised practice only. Never use examples to access another person's account, device or network without permission.

This policy may be updated when website features or practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.

For privacy-related questions, use the contact details on the Contact page if and when the site owner publishes them.
