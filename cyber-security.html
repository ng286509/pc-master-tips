Cyber Security Awareness

Learn how to recognise common online risks and protect your accounts, devices and personal information.

01. Identify Phishing Messages

  • Be careful with urgent messages asking you to act immediately.
  • Check the sender's full email address or phone number.
  • Hover over links on a computer to inspect the destination before opening.
  • Never enter passwords or OTPs after following an unexpected message link.

02. Create Strong Account Protection

  • Use a unique password for every important account.
  • Use a reputable password manager.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication wherever available.
  • Never share OTPs, recovery codes or password reset links.

03. Browse and Download Safely

  • Download software from official websites or trusted app stores.
  • Check file names and extensions before opening attachments.
  • Keep your browser, operating system and security software updated.
  • Avoid unknown USB drives and unexpected executable files.

04. Safe Learning Lab

Practise security concepts using dummy accounts, sample messages and systems you own or have explicit permission to test.

Do not collect real passwords, OTPs or personal data. Do not send deceptive links to other people. Keep demonstrations local and consent-based.

05. What To Do If You Clicked a Suspicious Link

  • Close the page and do not enter any information.
  • If you entered a password, change it from the official website using a trusted device.
  • Sign out other sessions and enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Run a security scan and report suspicious messages to the relevant service.
